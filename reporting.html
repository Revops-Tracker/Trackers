BDRs

Books: new bookings whose first slot falls in the period. Runs due: discovery calls whose slot arrived in the period. Runs had: due calls that were held. Overall show = runs had ÷ runs due. 1st-time show = new books held at their first slot ÷ new books whose first slot came due. MRR generated: monthly retainer (USD) of deals closed won in the period, credited to the rep who booked them. Designs (designs created): deals moved into the Design/Prop Review stage in HubSpot. Designs held: deals moved from Design on to a later stage (Prop Update or beyond).

AEs

Discos run: discovery calls held. Designs held: deals moved from Design to a later stage. Closed won revenue: monthly retainer (USD) by close date. Goals are weekly × the number of weeks in the period.
