BDRs

Books: new calls booked in the period, counted on the day they were booked (a rebook only counts as new when it is booked more than 30 days after the deal's last slot). Runs due: discovery calls whose slot arrived in the period, counted per call (a deal that no-shows and is rebooked into the same period = 2 calls due). Runs had: calls that were held. Overall show = runs had ÷ runs due. No-show recovery: of deals that missed a call in the period, the share held at a later slot (recent periods can still recover). 1st-time show = new books held at their first slot ÷ new books whose first slot came due in the period. MRR generated: monthly retainer (USD) of deals closed won in the period, credited to the rep who booked them. Designs (designs created): deals moved into the Design/Prop Review stage in HubSpot. Designs held: deals moved from Design on to a later stage (Prop Update or beyond).

AEs

Discos run: discovery calls held. Designs held: deals moved from Design to a later stage. Closed won revenue: monthly retainer (USD) by close date. Goals are weekly × the number of weeks in the period.
